Our Story

Born from the Grill, Made with Love

Ihawan — the Filipino word for 'to grill' — is more than a restaurant. It started as a family tradition: charcoal burning, skewers sizzling, neighbors gathering.

We brought that backyard spirit to a real kitchen, keeping every marinade, every technique, and every recipe rooted in authentic Filipino tradition. Every dish we serve carries the flavor of home.

🔥 100% Charcoal
🇵🇭 Authentic
